# A Masterclass in Structure: A Detailed Review of *Pulp Fiction* (1994)

Released in 1994, **Pulp Fiction** is widely considered the definitive example of a modern action-mystery thriller. Directed by **Quentin Tarantino** and written by **Quentin Tarantino**, the film is a sprawling, non-linear narrative that has captivated audiences for nearly three decades. It is a film that is as much a study in storytelling mechanics as it is a great piece of cinema.

### The Premise: A World Without a Clock
