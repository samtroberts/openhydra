Peer-to-peer networking is a decentralized system where individual devices, known as peers, connect directly to share resources, data, and peripherals without relying on a central server or router as a gateway. Unlike traditional networks, there is no central authority managing traffic, as each participant acts as both a client requesting resources and as a server providing them as needed. This architecture allows for flexible, overlapping, and overlapping networks where any device can act as a node, making it highly suitable for local, small-scale, or ad-hoc connections.
